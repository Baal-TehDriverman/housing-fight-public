## What the AG's investigation found (official record)
- 150+ complaints, 60 Hurst & Son parks statewide. Issues: "Lease terms, proper
  notice of rent increases, utilities, fees, and other issues."
- Dispute resolution was suspended; the Program AAG negotiated directly.
- Outcome: "Over $5.5 million reimbursed; ongoing Program services."

## The voluntary compliance measures (AG's words)
1. Modify leases/community rules — REMOVE "Landlord's Right of First Refusal"
   provision; revise maintenance obligations.
2. Reimburse tenants for improper rent increases or other charges.
3. ELIMINATE a $65 "notice fee" and an "excess water" charge — with reimbursements.
4. REDUCE a $65 extra vehicle fee and a $65 late rent fee — with reimbursements.
5. Centralized "Tenant Relations" system.
- Reimbursements "based on a tenant's rental history and varied"; INCLUDED
  non-complaining tenants and former tenants; mostly issued as rental credit.
- AG spokesperson: "Tenants were reimbursed the difference between any invalid rent
  increase and their original rent for each applicable month. Tenants were reimbursed
  in full for certain fees, and partial refunds on others. Private utilities/utilities
  assessed during the term of a rental agreement were reimbursed in full."
- 2/27/25 case entry: "Unilateral change of lease renewal date; Ineffective rent
  increases; non-compliant utility charges; Excessive fees. Voluntary Compliance."

## Mapping to Eric's facts (Space 24, Cedar Lane)
1. WATER: The "excess water" charge was ELIMINATED company-wide with reimbursements,
   and mid-term utility assessments were reimbursed IN FULL. If any water dollars sit
   inside the $1,743.23 notice, they match a charge the AG already killed. -> Itemize
   the notice; every water dollar is presumptively improper.
2. THE $43.23: $1,743.23 = ~$1,700 rent + ~$43.23 extras. The killed $65 notice fee and
   reduced $65 late fee are the prime suspects for that remainder. If either appears,
   it is a repeat violation of the company's own compliance agreement.
3. LEASE: Check Eric's signed lease for the "Landlord's Right of First Refusal"
   provision — it was supposed to be REMOVED from all Hurst leases. If present, the
   compliance measure was not implemented at Cedar Lane.
4. KARWAN PRECEDENT (same report): "$30,000+ back rent demands under threat of
   eviction" -> Cease and Desist Order. A 14-day notice used to collect disputed
   utility charges under threat of eviction follows the same pattern.
5. "Program services are ongoing" — the Hurst matter is NOT closed. A new MHDRP
   complaint joins a live enforcement stream, not a cold inbox.
6. Reimbursements reached non-complainers as rental credit — check Eric's ledger for
   credits he may have received without realizing.
