## Primary-text findings (RCW 59.20, read from app.leg.wa.gov 2026-09-24)
- **The rental agreement controls everything.** Termination for nonpayment is allowed only for
  "rent or other charges **specified in the rental agreement**" (59.20.080(1)(b)). Every dollar of
  the $1,743.23 must be traceable to the signed lot lease. Anything not in it cannot ground the notice.
- **Water mid-lease rule.** The agreement must list utilities and fees (59.20.060(1)(j)); if any
  utility is "changed to be charged independent of the rent during the term," the landlord must
  **decrease the rent proportionately**. Water added mid-term with no rent decrease = direct violation,
  and matches the AG's "reimbursed in full" category.
- **The notice's payment-method demand is challengeable.** "Cashier's check or money order only"
  vs. 59.20.134(1): a landlord **must accept a personal check** too, unless the tenant bounced one
  to the landlord in the previous nine months. If Eric never bounced a check, the restriction in
  the notice may itself violate the statute.
- **Late-fee caps** (59.20.060(2)(j), leases from 5/7/2025): 2% first month past due, 3% second,
  5% third+; no late fee at all within 5 days of due date. Audit the $1,743.23 for excess late fees.
- **Even if evicted: 120 days to sell in place** (59.20.080(3)), provided rent incurred after
  eviction stays current and arrears/fees are paid at assignment.
- **Catch (59.20.240):** tenant must be current on rent (incl. agreed utilities) before exercising
  chapter remedies — BUT the right to defend "no rent due and owing" in an unlawful detainer
  proceeding is expressly preserved.
- **Receipts:** written receipt mandatory for cash (59.20.134(2)); on request for any other form
  (59.20.134(3)). Always demand one.
- **Cedar Lane's own AG history:** 12/18/17, Cedar Lane Mobile Home Park, Sedro-Woolley —
  "Failure to maintain septic system," Voluntary Compliance. The park itself is a repeat player.
