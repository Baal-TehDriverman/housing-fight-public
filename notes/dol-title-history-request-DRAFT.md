## Notes and cautions

- **Sign and date in ink.** DOL "cannot process incomplete and unsigned forms."
- **DOL does not provide current titles or registration certificates**, and does
  not conduct titling services. This request is for **records only**.
- **DPPA applies** — disclosure is governed by 18 U.S.C. §2721 and RCW 46.12.635.
  Personal information may be redacted.
- **The VIN is the load-bearing field.** Double-check it against the contract
  (page 1: `01700PHA004036A`) and the assessor record before submitting.

---

## What to do with the result

- If the history shows **only Ron'Cal Homes LLC** — the ownership question is
  settled against a government record, and the "buying on contract" claim rests
  on the contract plus the assessor plus DOL.
- If it shows **an earlier owner** — the "Condition: [New]" representation in
  the contract is contradicted, and the manufacturer's warranty timeline moves.
- Either way, file it in `evidence/` and cite the DOL record number.
