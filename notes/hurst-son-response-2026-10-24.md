## The three contradictions

### (a) Late fee: letter vs lease vs statute

| Source | Late fee |
|---|---|
| **Landlord's letter, 2026-10-24** | "capped at 1.5% of rent" = **$12.75** |
| **Signed lot lease, §5** | **$20.00** if not received by the 8th, **+$2.00/day**, cap **$60.00** |
| **RCW 59.20.060(2)(j)** (HB 1217, 2025 c. 209) | **2%** first month, **3%** second, **5%** third+ = **$17 / $25.50 / $42.50** |

**The 1.5% figure is a myth.** It appeared in an early House draft of HB 1217
and was **removed before enactment**. The enacted caps for manufactured/mobile
home lot tenancies are 2/3/5 percent.

The lease's own escape hatch, §5:
> "unless otherwise defined by local governance, in which **the more restrictive
> late fee will take precedence**."

Lease says $60. Statute says $17. Their letter says $12.75. Most restrictive
controls. **Any late fee above $12.75 is uncollectible on their own written
admission.** They charged exactly $12.75 — nothing to claw back yet, but the
admission is now in writing, on their letterhead, from their counsel's office.

### (b) RUBS method: two different systems described

**Lease §9:**
> "Any utility in which all lot spaces within the community are not sub-metered
> will be calculated by a Ratio Utility Billing System (RUBS) to charge tenants
> for their proportionate share of the Landlord's actual utility costs by
> **dividing its actual utility costs evenly among each occupied space**."

**Landlord's letter, same envelope:**
> "Cedar Lane Mobile Estates LLC uses **your total consumption** to calculate
> **your percentage** of the respective bill."

**Even division among occupied spaces** and **consumption-based percentage** are
not the same method. One of the two documents is wrong about how he is billed.

### (c) The utility "itemization" does not reach Lot 24

The `Cedar Lane Utilities.pdf` is **18 pages of Skagit PUD master bills** for the
entire community — "RES MULTI," amounts $2,219–$2,691/month, dated Apr–Jun 2025.
**No per-lot breakdown, no allocation schedule, no reference to Lot 24, no
Peavey Rd, no Hill.**

The letter claims this "resolves request 5." It does not. It shows what the
community owes the PUD. It does not show how Lot 24's share is computed, what the
occupied-space denominator is, or what the percentage is. **The billing method
remains unverified.**
