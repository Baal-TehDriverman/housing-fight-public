# OFFENSE PLAYBOOK — researched 2026-09-24, all from primary sources

## Weapon 1: MHDRP complaint (RCW 59.30.040) — FILE BEFORE THEY FILE IN COURT
- Any aggrieved party may file with the AG alleging a 59.20 violation.
- AG investigates with subpoena power (30-day response), facilitates negotiation.
- No agreement -> written determination. Violation found -> notice of violation,
  15 business days to correct, then **$250/day/violation fines**.
- AG may order cease & desist + affirmative action **including REFUNDS of improper
  fees, charges, assessments** (59.30.040(7)(a)).
- TIMING (59.30.040(13)): once an unlawful detainer is filed/served, MHDRP relief
  narrows — BUT a tenant may still seek relief if the complaint alleges the notice
  of termination violates 59.20.080, filed BEFORE the court action. File now.
- Precedent against THIS company: AG's 2023-24 Hurst investigation -> $5.5M refunded
  Sep 2024-Jan 2025 (~2,700 tenants, avg ~$2,000, some $10,000+). Refund formula per
  AG spokesperson: invalid rent increases reimbursed per month; certain fees in full;
  **"private utilities/utilities assessed during the term of a rental agreement
  were reimbursed in full."** Formula applied to similarly-situated tenants who never
  complained. (Cascade PBS, 1/30/2025; AG case-outcomes page 2/27/25 entry.)
- Eric's water-charge allegation (lease says included -> billed mid-term) matches a
  refund category dollar-for-dollar.

## Weapon 2: Cure the arrears = load the weapon (RCW 59.20.240)
- Tenant must be current on rent/agreed utilities to exercise chapter remedies.
- $1,743.23 cure via emergency rental assistance (211 / Community Action of Skagit
  County) is not surrender — it unlocks every offensive remedy.

## Weapon 3: CPA lawsuit (RCW 19.86.090)
- Private action: actual damages + costs + reasonable attorney's fee; court MAY treble
  (cap $25,000 for 19.86.020 deceptive-act claims). Fee-shifting -> contingency counsel.
- Candidate claims: $65,000 (Feb 2024) -> $91,835 total contract on a $56,000 home;
  "brand new" representation (title history pending); undisclosed Hurst/Ron'Cal/Cedar
  Lane affiliation; balloon structure; possible TILA/contract disclosure defects.

## Weapon 4: MHLTA fee-shifting (RCW 59.20.110)
- "In any action arising out of this chapter, the prevailing party shall be entitled
  to reasonable attorney's fees and costs." Contingency lawyers know this statute.

## Weapon 5: Diminished rental value + set-off (RCW 59.20.220)
- If landlord failed duties under 59.20.130 after tenant notice (59.20.200: 24hr/48hr/
  7-day/30-day clocks), court may award rent paid in excess of diminished rental value
  **as a set-off against the landlord's claims** — offense that directly eats arrears.
- 59.20.200 notice sets this up. Serve written notice specifying property, owner,
  defective condition.

## Retaliation caveat (RCW 59.20.075) — be straight about it
- Filing within 120 days AFTER the landlord's notice can trigger a presumption the
  complaint was not in good faith. Frame MHDRP complaint as enforcement of existing
  rights + direct challenge to the notice under 59.20.080 (expressly preserved by
  59.30.040(13)), not as retaliation.
