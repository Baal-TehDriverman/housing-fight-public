### The defect: the notice's own arithmetic does not work

> Dated **September 24, 2026**. Requires payment "on or before, and not later
> than **10/17/2026**, **Fourteen (14)** days henceforth."

**Fourteen days from September 24 is October 8.** The notice gives **twenty-three
days**. Either the printed date is wrong or the "14 days" is. A notice that must
be exact is internally inconsistent.

*(Flagged in the repo README on 2026-09-24 as "recheck document before legal
reliance." Now confirmed against the image.)*

### Two more notes

- **"The purpose of this correspondence is an attempt to collect a debt."**
  Debt-collection language in a pay-or-vacate notice. Worth noting.
- **Payment method.** RCW 59.20.134(1) requires a landlord to accept a personal
  check unless the tenant has bounced one to the landlord in the previous nine
  months. No bounced check is in the record.

## 2. Rent and Fee Increase Notice to Tenants (MHLTA)

**Source:** [`../attachments/2026-09-25-rent-increase/2026-09-25-rent-increase-page1-clean.jpg`](../attachments/2026-09-25-rent-increase/2026-09-25-rent-increase-page1-clean.jpg)

**This is the official Washington state form** — "RENT AND FEE INCREASE NOTICE
TO TENANTS (MHLTA)" — and it cites **Section 201 and Section 202, Chapter 209,
Laws of 2025 (WA)**, which is **HB 1217**.

> Your landlord, **Cedar Lane Mobile Estates LLC**, intends to (check one of
> the following):
>
> **X** Raise your rent and/or other recurring and periodic charges: Your total
> increase in rent and other recurring and periodic charges effective
> **(11/1/2026)** will be **(3.00%)**, which totals an additional **($25.50)**
> per month, for a new total amount of **($875.50)** per month for rent and
> other recurring or periodic charges.
>
> This increase in rent and/or other recurring or periodic charges is allowed by
> state law and is (check one of the following):
>
> **X** A lower increase than the maximum allowed by state law.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Old rent (derived) | **$850.00** |
| Increase | **$25.50 / month** |
| Percentage | **3.00%** |
| New total | **$875.50 / month** |
| Effective | **11/1/2026** |
| Statutory basis | HB 1217, ch. 209, Laws of 2025 |
| Exemption claimed | **None** |

### Correction to the repo's earlier framing

The AG complaint's subject line calls the matter a "defective rent-increase
notice." **That is correct.** There *is* a rent-increase notice — served the day
after the pay-or-vacate — and it is a real, separate document. The $1,743.23 is
arrears; the rent increase is additional. Both are in play.

*(An earlier note in this repo treated "rent-increase notice" as a
mischaracterization of the arrears demand. That was wrong and is corrected
here.)*
