## Method and limits (read this first)

- Each manufactured home in the park has its own personal-property parcel (mostly
  P6782x/P678xx series; newer installs use P1xxxxx series).
- The assessor page shows only the LATEST recorded transfer per home. It does NOT
  show the full sale chain.
- The assessor's excise-affidavit endpoint returned an error on 2026-09-25, so
  historical transfers, hold times, rapid resales, and seller identity (bank/REO/
  corporate) cannot yet be verified from public pages alone.
- Results below are a LOWER BOUND: homes whose currently displayed latest transfer
  falls in a period. A home bought in 2020 and resold in 2025 shows only the 2025
  transfer.
- Coverage: 39 of 47 spaces. Missing spaces (no indexed assessor page found):
  11, 31, 38, 42, 44, 45, 46, 47. These may be vacant, park-owned, or unindexed —
  not verified.

## Answer to the research question

Yes — the public record shows substantial recent movement. Nearly two-thirds of
the homes with assessor records changed hands (latest transfer) since 2021, and
nearly three-quarters since 2016. That is a high-turnover park by the visible
measure.

What this does NOT yet prove:
- Deliberate churn, flipping, or repossession by park-affiliated entities.
- Any rapid resale (two sales of the same home within ~3 years) — sale chains are
  unavailable; the excise-affidavit endpoint errored.
- Bank/REO/corporate sellers — the current-owner pages don't show seller identity.
