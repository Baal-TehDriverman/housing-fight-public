## Three things worth noting

**1. The Lessor's address is the park.**
"Ron 'Cal' Homes, LLC ... whose address is **Cedar Lane Mobile Estates LLC**,
Cedar Lane Mobile Estates, Sedro-Woolley, WA" The home seller's contract address **is** the land landlord.
That is the common-control link, in the operator's own document.

**2. "Condition: [New]"** on a 2023 model, recorded sold 2024-02-12 at $65,000.
Consistent with new old stock off a dealer lot — supports the "brand new"
representation.

**3. The $500 is explained.** The sample terms Igor Oliveira sent on Oct 10
listed a "$500 Advance Payment due at signing" alongside the "$5,000
consideration." $5,000 + $500 = **$5,500** — the amount Schrier demanded on
Oct 30. Not unexplained money.
