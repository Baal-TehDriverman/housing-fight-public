## Notes for Eric (not part of the complaint)
- Complaints are NOT anonymous — the AG notifies the landlord. That's intentional here: under RCW 59.20.075, a landlord action listed in RCW 59.20.070(5) (eviction, termination, non-renewal) within 120 days of your good-faith complaint creates a rebuttable presumption it was retaliatory — the burden shifts to them. File BEFORE Oct 17 so the presumption covers the notice deadline.
- The presumption is rebuttable, not absolute: they can still argue pure nonpayment. The water-charge dispute (RCW 59.20.070(6)) is what attacks the "nonpayment" amount itself.
- The AG's own guidance: you don't need to cite statutes. Short plain narrative is what they want; staff determines violations. (Statute cites above are for your reference.)
