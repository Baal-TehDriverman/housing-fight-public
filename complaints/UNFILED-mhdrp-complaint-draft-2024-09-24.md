## Alleged violations of RCW 59.20 (MHLTA)
A. RCW 59.20.080(1)(b): a 14-day notice may demand only "rent or other charges
   SPECIFIED IN THE RENTAL AGREEMENT." Utility charges added during the lease term
   and fees not in the agreement cannot support the notice.
B. RCW 59.20.060(1)(a),(j): lease must state rent, charges, utilities, services,
   fees. If a utility became separately charged during the term, rent must decrease
   proportionately.
C. RCW 59.20.070(6): utility fees may not exceed actual cost.
D. RCW 59.20.134: landlord must accept personal check, cashier's check, or money
   order — a cashier's-check-ONLY demand is suspect (case law needed; plead as
   allegation, not conclusion).
