## Why it matters

**Concession #1 — the itemization.** Requests 1–4 and 7 of the AG complaint are
answered. The ledger they had refused to produce is now produced.

**Concession #2 — the admission.**

> "any late fees are capped at **1.5% of the rent amount**"

**1.5% is a myth** — it was in an early House draft of HB 1217 and removed before
enactment. The enacted caps are **2% / 3% / 5%**. Their own lease says **$20–$60**.
The letter says **$12.75**. The lease's §5 says "the more restrictive late fee
will take precedence." **The admission is in writing, on their letterhead, from
their counsel's office.**

**Concession #3 — the lease.** A signed document, in their possession, supplied
only now.

**The gap.** Everything sent is **lot-side**. No Ron'Cal contract, no Ron'Cal
ledger, no receipt for the $5,500. The home-side documents carry the no-credit
and no-down-payment clauses — and they are the ones not produced.
