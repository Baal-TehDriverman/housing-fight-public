## Why this thread is load-bearing

See [`../notes/loan-thread-analysis.md`](../notes/loan-thread-analysis.md) for
the full analysis. In short:

1. A **mortgage application was submitted** — not a rental inquiry.
2. The lender called it a **purchase** twice, in writing: "pre approve you for a
   home loan," "this home purchase."
3. The mortgage was **denied on income documentation** (two simultaneous jobs for
   24 months) — not on credit, not on the property, not on the seller.
4. The lender's written instruction for what to do next was to **obtain seller
   financing through Amelia Campbell, then refinance out of it.**
5. Amelia Campbell's email of **October 10, 2024** says the same thing:
   > "Your goal should be to refinance when you can — that will spread the
   > balance over 25 years and you won't have a balloon-payment..."
6. The contract was signed **November 1, 2024** — three weeks after the lender
   named the route.
7. The "Lease with Option to Purchase" with Ron'Cal Homes LLC **is** that seller
   financing. It contains §7(b) ("does not constitute a down payment") and
   §7(c) ("No credit shall be given").
8. Eric had the down payment: he wrote, in his own hand, **"I do have 9-10%."**
   The lender required 5%. He paid **$5,500** on October 30, 2024.
