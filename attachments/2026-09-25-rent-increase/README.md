## Reading notes for counsel — the date problem

- The folder is named `2026-09-25-…`, which may record when the
  notice was PHOTOGRAPHED, not when it was dated or served.
- The exemptions page's own date line reads April 10, 2026. April 10
  → November 1 is roughly seven months — comfortably past the
  three-month notice period in RCW 59.20.090(2) — SO IF the packet
  was served in April, the notice period is compliant on its face.
- IF the packet was instead first served in September 2026 (37 days
  before Nov 1), the three-month arithmetic fails. **Service date is
  UNPROVEN in this repo. Do not plead a notice-period violation
  until service is established** (see
  `notes/fact-check-2026-10-24.md` §2.3 and
  `notes/corrections-2026-10-28.md` item 12).
- Do not assume the April-dated exemptions page belongs to the
  September packet: it may be a leftover page from an earlier
  packet. Treat the two pages as possibly-distinct documents until
  the complete packet and its service are confirmed.
- On RATE, the increase is facially compliant (3% < 5% cap, no
  exemption claimed). The repo's "defective rent-increase notice"
  subject line proves only that a rent-increase notice EXISTS.
- Transcription: `../../notes/notices-transcription-2026-09.md`
  (accurate; its timeline table correctly separates the Apr 10 date
  line from the Sep 25 file name).
- Guide: `../../LAWYER-README.md` §§3, 5 (item 8), 8 (item 6)
