  FOR ONE OR MORE OF THE FOLLOWING REASONS AND ARE SUBJECT TO BEING
  TOWED OR BOOTED AT YOUR EXPENSE") on resident vehicles on Oct 1,
  2026, and at least one work truck photographed without a sticker
  the same morning. This supports "a sticker sweep occurred on
  Oct 1" — the date Eric photographed them.

  violation checkbox is legibly checked, and the "BY" (issuer) field

  photos — several stickered resident cars and one unstickered work
  truck — do not establish that work trucks were systematically
  exempt or that residents were targeted. The sweep's legal basis
  (which park rule or lease provision was allegedly violated) is
  not established by the photos; compare any alleged violation
