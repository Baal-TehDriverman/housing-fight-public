## Reading notes for counsel

- **The 23-day span is NOT a defect.** Sep 24 → Oct 17 is 23 days,
  more than the 14-day statutory minimum. A notice giving more time
  than required benefits the tenant. Older notes calling this a
  "defect" are corrected by `notes/fact-check-2026-10-24.md` §2.1
  and `notes/corrections-2026-10-28.md` item 8. The operative
  deadline is the landlord's own October 17 date.
- **The payment-method restriction IS a live issue.** "Money order,
  or cashier's check only" excludes personal checks, which RCW
  59.20.134(1) requires a landlord to accept (absent a bounced check
  to the landlord/agent within the previous nine months — none is in
  the record). The October statements say "checks/money orders (NO
  CASH)" — a broader instruction than this notice's.
- **Service is unproven.** No certificate of service, mailing
  record, or receipt date appears on the photograph. Early notes
  saying "received ~Sep 22" are unsourced and inconsistent with the
  Sep 24 date line — do not use Sep 22.
- **The $1,743.23 decodes exactly** from the landlord's ledger
  (Aug balance $857.52 + Sep rent $850 + Sep water $22.96 + Sep late
  fee $12.75). The notice itself contains no itemization.
- **This is a 14-DAY notice.** Skagit Legal Aid's Oct 28, 2026
  email asks for a "30-Day Notice to Pay or Vacate." Confirm whether
  Leanne Canonizado means this notice or whether a separate 30-day
  notice exists — do not assume a second notice exists, and do not
  assume she means this one.
- **Cure status after Oct 17, 2026: UNKNOWN.** Do not state that
  Eric paid or did not pay.
