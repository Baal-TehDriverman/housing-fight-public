# Pre-screening application — 2024-11-01

**File:** `pre-screening-application.jpg` (photograph of the park's
pre-screening form, with handwriting).

## What it shows

The park's pre-screening checklist, including:

> "Type of Sale (Cash out VS Buying on Contract) — Find out if applicants
> are buying home on contract or paying it off. If contract, prev tenant
> and homeowner must be on a Lease with new applicants till home is paid
> off and new tenants can provide a proof of ownership transfer."

Handwritten below that bullet: **"buying on contract."** Other handwritten
marks: "1" under occupants, a mark under pets, "1" under vehicles.

## What it proves — and what it does not

- It proves the park's own form framed the transaction type as "buying
  on contract" as one of two options (cash out vs. buying on contract).
- It does NOT prove who wrote "buying on contract." The handwriting's
  author is unidentified in the repo. If Eric wrote it, it proves HIS
  understanding, not an operator representation. Establish authorship
  before relying on it as a misrepresentation (see
  `notes/corrections-2026-10-28.md` item 19).
- Note the form's own qualifier: under a contract, the previous tenant
  and homeowner "must be on a Lease with new applicants till home is
  paid off" — the park's paperwork itself anticipates a lease during
  the contract period. The deception argument therefore rests on what
  Eric was told the arrangement WAS (a purchase path), not on this
  form alone.
- The blank version of this form is also in the repo root as
  `attachments/Prescreening-Application.pdf` (see
  `attachments/README.md`).
