# Park documents — repo root (`attachments/`)

These four PDFs were sent with the October 10, 2024 park-rules email
(see `../../emails/2024-10-10-amelia-campbell-park-rules.md`) and sit
at the root of `attachments/` rather than in a dated subfolder.

## Files

- **`CL-Rules-and-Regs.pdf`** (12 pp., text layer) — Cedar Lane
  Mobile Estates LLC Rules and Regulations. By their own terms,
  "expressly made a part of the Rental Agreement." Notable
  provisions for counsel: Rule 2 (no renting, loaning, sub-letting,
  assignment, or transient use); Site Requirements (tenant maintains
  the lot; landscaping within 60 days or the landlord may hire a
  landscaper for up to $2,000 charged to the tenant); Rule 3
  Utilities (temporary disconnection for repairs with NO rent
  offset; tampering with community electrical/water/sewer
  forbidden); Water (check valve; winterize; wasteful-water
  Notice of Non-Compliance "subject to the fee associated with
  such a notice" — amount not stated in the excerpt); Septic/Sewer
  (sub-metered homes limited to 9,000 gal/month; **$150 overage
  fee** if exceeded; "For additional rules, please refer to Septic
  Agreement" — Eric's septic agreement is NOT in the repo); Guest
  parking (passenger cars/pickups only; a guest vehicle parked over
  36 hours must be identified by resident name + space # "to
  prevent towing or impounding"). **Counsel should read the full
  12 pages** — the vehicle rules and any fee schedule in the rules
  bear on the Oct 1, 2026 parking sweep and on any fee claim.
- **`New-Tenant-Info-Sheet.pdf`** — new-tenant information sheet
  (move-in packet).
- **`Orca-Application.pdf`** — tenant screening application
  (the $60 application fee is referenced in the Oct 10, 2024 email;
  whether Eric paid it, and by what instrument, is not shown in the
  bank records in this repo).
- **`Prescreening-Application.pdf`** — the BLANK prescreening form.
  The HANDWRITTEN version (with "buying on contract" entered) is
  photographed separately in
  `2024-11-01-pre-screening/pre-screening-application.jpg` — see
  that folder's README for the authorship caveat.
