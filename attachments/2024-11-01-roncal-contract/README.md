## What §§5–7 establish

- §5 Expenses Incurred by Lessor: lessor's costs (tax, assessment,
  insurance premium, maintenance, repair, filing/recording fees) are
  reimbursed by lessee on demand, else added to rent, bearing interest
  at the **Default Rate** — which is defined in the missing pages.
- §6 Taxes: lessor pays taxes/license fees/government charges on
  notice; lessee reimburses as directed, else interest at the Default
  Rate. Excise/sales/use taxes on transfer of title are the lessee's
  if the option is exercised.
- §7 Option to Purchase: $5,000 consideration, receipt acknowledged.
  (a) Exercise only within ninety (90) days, and not sooner, of the
  Initial Term's termination — or, if renewed, within ninety (90)
  days, and not sooner, of the Renewal Term's termination — by
  written notice. (b) The $5,000 "**does not constitute a down
  payment and is non-refundable** upon execution." (c) If exercised
  in the Initial Term window: **$25,000 cash at closing**; "Any
  portion of this amount may be paid in advance at signing. **No
  credit shall be given** for the consideration paid for the option
  by Lessee or for Lease payments received by the Lessor." Closing
  no later than sixty (60) days after exercise notice. (d) If
  renewed and exercised in the Renewal Term window: **$3,000 cash
  at closing** (sentence continues beyond the photograph).
